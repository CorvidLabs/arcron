# arcron

Every serious chain should let you say "call this later" without running a server, and Algorand has no way to do it. Arcron should be that: a schedule that lives on chain, paid for out of escrow, executed by whoever turns up, and outliving whoever created it. It should feel like handing a job to infrastructure rather than to a person — no allowlist, no stake, no token, no relationship with a keeper — while being completely honest that reliability here is economic rather than technical, and that an upkeep only runs while somebody finds it worth running. Everything it claims should be checkable with a command, and what is unproven should be said as loudly as what is proven.
