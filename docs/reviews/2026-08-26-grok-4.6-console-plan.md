## 1. What the plan gets wrong, ranked

### 1. The NFD map is the product, and it has never been seen

Confirmed: the plan copies five destinations, a left sidebar, and global search from NFDomains (`docs/console-plan.md:18-38`, `docs/journeys.md:23-42`). Confirmed: the console today is two tabs and a pinned form, with no router (`web/src/app/app.ts:33-38`, `web/src/app/app.html:18-41`, `web/src/app/app.config.ts:3-4`).

NFDomains sells names. One field, one button, a marketplace of uniquely named things. Arcron sells a scheduled inner call paid from escrow. The register form is nine controls plus a policy fieldset (`register-form.ts:47-121`). Upkeeps have no names (`docs/ac/j3-j4.md:484-487`). The live registry is five boxes.

Copying NFD's chrome onto that is not "the shape, corrected." It is an unvalidated analogy doing the job of an information architecture. Dashboard, Marketplace, Analytics, and Manage will be four rooms around five rows. Global search over a set with no free text is a search box whose placeholder has to apologise for itself.

The maintainer already named this as the biggest unvalidated assumption. The plan then treats the analogy as decided. That is the cheap-now, expensive-later item: once those routes and that sidebar exist, every later journey is fitted to them.

### 3. "Lateness across the registry is exactly keeper responsiveness" is false

Confirmed: round pace is measured (`arcron.service.ts:115-125`, `RATE_SAMPLES = 48`). Confirmed: median lateness is computed over every upkeep classified `due` (`js/src/board.ts:107-124`). Confirmed: `due` means funded and past `nextExecutionRound` (`js/src/board.ts:49-56`). It does not mean a keeper could have executed it.

Lateness in this registry mixes keeper absence, targets that revert (keepers then back off: `docs/integrating.md:90-98`), cadences the live keeper was never going to hit, and upkeeps that have never run. The honest sentence the plan wants ("keepers here are running about 20 minutes behind, so expect roughly that") will be dominated by the worst of those, not by keeper SLA.

The TestNet keeper is a half-hourly cron (`.github/workflows/keeper-bot.yml:37`, `docs/hosting.md:56-68`). The form defaults to 10 rounds, about 28 seconds (`register-form.ts:192-195`, `MIN_INTERVAL_ROUNDS`). Hosting.md says the shortest live cadence is about six hours and that half-hourly is late by at most eight percent of one interval. The console's default is a different product from the network it will describe.

I did not re-read the live registry. `docs/ac/j3-j4.md:118-122` recorded, at one round, 5 upkeeps, all due, 4 never run, median lateness 5,785 rounds. If that is still roughly true, the plan's sentence would currently say "expect hours," which is not "keeper responsiveness."

### 5. The Test button is specified as a feeling, not as a call

The idea is right. A real execution's inner call is an `itxn.ApplicationCall` from the keeper app account (`contract.py:462-478`). `docs/integrating.md:49-61` tells integrators to assert `Txn.sender == Application(keeper).address`. Testing as the user is a confident wrong answer.

The plan then says "Free, no signature, no state change" (`docs/console-plan.md:76-79`) and stops.

Confirmed: you cannot `execute` an upkeep that does not exist. `execute` asserts the box (`contract.py:368-371`). The Test button therefore cannot reuse `txns.execute`. It has to synthesise the inner call, with the app account as sender, unnamed resources allowed, and the same resource discovery `discoverResources` already does (`keeper-txns.ts:294-335`). That is not free in the engineering sense. If it is implemented as "simulate a call from the connected wallet," it becomes the bug the plan exists to prevent.

The attestation does not gate on the test (`docs/console-plan.md:81-83`). Combined with no target lookup (the contract does not check `target_app` exists: `contract.py:171-238`; the form only has `Validators.min(1)` at `register-form.ts:187`), the expensive first-user failure is still "escrow against a typo, watch nothing, cancel to get most of it back." For a permissionless network that is philosophically clean. For the primary surface it is how J2 fails after the form looks honest.

## 3. What is over-built, and the three cuts

**The demo target cut is right,** with one condition. A toy upkeep as the first action trains "this calls nothing I care about." Linking out to `docs/integrating.md` is honest. The condition: J1 must actually show a serviced network, and the integration guide must be completable without asking you. Status still lists "can someone get an upkeep running from the docs alone?" as unknown (`docs/status.md:74`). If J1 is a quiet registry and J2 starts after a contract, a stranger cannot try it and cannot see it working either.

**The leaderboard cut is right.** The chain records that an upkeep ran, not who (`js/src/board.ts:10-13`). `web/README.md:52-74` already took this decision. Do not reopen it.

**The elaborate verification cut is right while the app is unfrozen.** A hash in the bundle is the page asserting its own integrity. An honest `update` would make a compiled expected hash lie until the bundle is rebuilt. The freeze warning is the true statement (`trust-banner.ts:74-84`, and the live app is not frozen: `docs/status.md:26`). What must not be cut with it: hostile app-id warning (keep), explorer links (add), full app account (add), superseded-id tier (add). Those are not self-verification.

**Cut more:**

- **Clone to MainNet.** Design it the week MainNet is a console network with a published app id. Not now.
- **Analytics as a destination.** Two rates are a sentence, not a section. Median lateness already exists as a tile (`upkeep-board.ts:63`).
- **Global search in the chrome.** Search belongs inside the registry when the list needs it. Five named boxes do not.
- **Dashboard "recent executions."** Last-serviced round is in the box (`upkeep.ts:50-51, 141`). Transaction ids of past executions are not, except what this browser sent, unless you fetch blocks the way the notifier does (`scripts/notifier.py:46-48`, bounded to 24). A feed is an indexer-shaped feature in a no-indexer product. Show "last ran" on the upkeep. Do not invent a feed.
- **Three signature fields,** unless you also fetch an ABI from somewhere. Otherwise keep one string and put the effort into the Test button.
- **Five NFD destinations.** Ship a dashboard sentence, a register route, a registry, and `/u/:id`. Manage can be a Mine chip. Health can be a line on the dashboard.

**Do not cut:** the Test-as-Arcron idea, cost honesty, spendable-balance block, expected-outcome copy, unserviced as its own word, fee-immutability copy at register, landing on the upkeep page, Run now as permissionless execute (the control already exists: `registry-table.ts:120-126`, `upkeep-board.ts:85-91`; the work is language and placement).

**The cut that would be wrong:** killing Run now once a keeper is "coming." A permissionless network's honest control is "anyone may take this fee." Hide it and you teach the wrong story. The plan is right to keep it always.

## 4. The build order, and what I would change

The plan's five steps:

1. Default to TestNet  
2. Router and a page per upkeep  
3. Register-form honesty  
4. Run now, and the two live rates  
5. Search, filters with counts, mine  

**Step 3 does not depend on step 5.** Confirmed. Search and Mine are not inputs to cost, signature, Test, or attestation.

**Step 3 does depend on step 4's rates,** if "how long the funding lasts" and "when this will run" are part of honesty. Today the runway is priced in round-rate time (`register-form.ts:300-309`). Without keeper lateness, the form still promises ~1 min 24 s on a half-hourly network. The plan puts that truth in step 4, after people can already register at the wrong expectation. Fee and ceiling cannot be changed later (`contract.py` has no such method). The irreversible step is using step 4's information.

**Step 4's Run now depends on step 2's page** if that is where it lives. The execute machinery already lives on the table and the board. You can put honest language on those without a router. The first-run journey ("watch it execute without hunting") does need the page.

**Step 2 is sequenced as narrative completeness** (register, then see the thing) **ahead of irreversibility** (wrong quote, untested target, ASA trap). That is the wrong order for a money surface.

**What I would do instead:**

0. **Reconcile the documents.** Rewrite J2's "Done when," J5's hash line, and `docs/ac/` so they match the decisions. Until that happens, the plan is not executable. This is the same class of bug as a fix at one call site.

1. **Front door, for real.** `DEFAULT_NETWORK = 'testnet'`, `web-build-hosted` in a lane, a URL in `docs/status.md` and the README, explorer link helpers that something actually calls. Flip the `entry.test.ts:35-38` pin. LocalNet via `?network=localnet`, as decided.

2. **Money honesty on the form that exists.** Named cost lines including group fees, spendable balance, hide ASA or finish it, Test as the inner call from the app account with `discoverResources`, attestation, simulate register before the wallet, `MAX_CALL_DATA`, fee-cannot-change copy, default cadence and funding that survive contact with the half-hourly keeper. Do this before adding routes. The cross-field validation is the best part of the console (`register-form.ts:209-223`, `docs/journeys.md:109-111`). Moving the form while rewriting it is how that gets lost (`docs/ac/j3-j4.md:715-718`).

3. **The upkeep page, with `entry.ts` still owning network and app.** Register lands there. Explorer links. Refund preview. Top-up gift wording. Run now as execute, in those words. Expected outcomes parsed from the node string. Unserviced ported from `STALL_INTERVALS`. This is J2's ending and J3's start.

4. **Two rates, with a correct definition.** Round pace as already measured. Keeper responsiveness as lateness among upkeeps that are funded *and* whose last Test (or last successful run) says the target works, not median overdue of everything `due`. Put the sentence on the form and on the page.

5. **Mine as a chip** (connected wallet as the one-press shortcut; pasting an address in search can wait). Search and filter counts when the list is long enough to need them. Not five destinations.

Clone, Analytics, global search, recent-executions feed: not in this sequence.
