# Deciding whether the idea is any good

## Intent

Somebody should be able to decide in ten minutes whether this is worth anything, and the case should be built so that they can check it rather than take it. That means pricing it against the cost of owning a bot instead of against a single transaction, naming the free alternatives that genuinely win, saying where the argument stops being true, and listing what you give up rather than claiming you give up nothing. The most useful thing this project can publish is the test that would settle it against itself, and somewhere for a reader who decided not to use it to say why.

## Criteria

- **CASE-1**  I can tell in one minute what this is for.
  - **CASE-1.a**  I am told why the chain does not already have it.
- **CASE-2**  The front door branches by what I came to do rather than explaining itself in its own order.
  - **CASE-2.a**  Each branch hands me to one document rather than to a tour.
- **CASE-3**  The cost is compared against the cost of owning a bot, not against the cost of one transaction.
- **CASE-4**  The free alternatives are named, including the ones that genuinely beat it.
  - **CASE-4.a**  A scheduler that does not fire on the schedule it was given is measured rather than dismissed.
- **CASE-5**  I am told where this stops being the cheaper option and running my own bot wins.
- **CASE-6**  I am told what I give up, in specifics, rather than being told I give up nothing.
- **CASE-7**  I am told what it cannot do — it cannot fetch anything from the world — as clearly as what it can.
- **CASE-8**  The earlier attempts at this are recorded, including what they did differently and what became of them.
- **CASE-9**  The one outcome that would settle whether this is real infrastructure is written down, so I can hold the project to it.
- **CASE-10**  I can read the whole thing in one ordered pass rather than hopping between pages.
  - **CASE-10.a**  Where that read disagrees with the documents it was compiled from, the documents win.
- **CASE-11**  There is somewhere to say why I looked and decided not to use it.
  - **CASE-11.a**  That is wanted more than a bug report is.
